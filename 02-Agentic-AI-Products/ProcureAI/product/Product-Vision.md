# ProcureAI — Product Vision

## 1. Product Overview

**Product Name:** ProcureAI

**Product Category:** Enterprise Agentic AI Procurement Platform

**Product Stage:** Concept / Prototype

**Target Users:** Enterprise employees, procurement teams, purchasing managers, and approvers.

## 2. Problem Statement

Enterprise procurement often requires employees to navigate multiple systems to identify products, compare suppliers, validate budgets, obtain approvals, and create purchase requisitions.

These fragmented processes introduce manual effort, inconsistent purchasing experiences, and potential compliance risks.

## 3. Product Vision

To simplify enterprise procurement through an intelligent, governed AI assistant that transforms natural-language purchasing requests into validated business transactions.

## 4. Value Proposition

ProcureAI aims to:

- Reduce manual effort during purchase requisition preparation.
- Improve access to approved catalogs and supplier information.
- Support informed purchasing decisions.
- Enforce procurement policies and authorization requirements.
- Provide transparent, auditable AI-assisted workflows.

## 5. Why Agentic AI?

Conventional automation works well when inputs and workflows are predefined.

Agentic AI can add value by interpreting natural-language requests, resolving ambiguity, retrieving information from different sources, selecting appropriate tools, and adapting information-gathering steps to the purchasing context.

Financial approvals, authorization, and final transaction execution will remain controlled by deterministic business services.

## 6. MVP Scope

The initial prototype will support:

1. Natural-language purchase requests.
2. Material and catalog search.
3. Approved supplier identification and comparison.
4. Budget validation.
5. Procurement policy checks.
6. Human approval where required.
7. Simulated purchase requisition creation.
8. Execution logs and audit history.

## 7. Success Metrics

The prototype will evaluate:

- Purchase request interpretation accuracy.
- Catalog matching accuracy.
- End-to-end workflow completion rate.
- Procurement policy compliance.
- Transaction execution reliability.
- Average processing latency.
- AI model cost per completed request.

Baseline measurements and target thresholds will be defined before formal testing.

## 8. Product Roadmap

**Phase 1:** Single-agent prototype with enterprise tools.

**Phase 2:** Multi-agent implementation with specialized catalog and sourcing capabilities.

**Phase 3:** Comparative evaluation of accuracy, cost, latency, and reliability.

**Phase 4:** GitHub portfolio publication and LinkedIn case study.

## 9. Key Product Principle

ProcureAI will distinguish between AI-driven reasoning and enterprise-controlled actions.

The AI agent may interpret requests, retrieve information, and recommend decisions, but backend services will enforce purchasing permissions, budgets, supplier eligibility, approvals, and transaction integrity.
